Shipping Policy

SDK Masale currently ships only within Maharashtra. We are working on expanding delivery to the rest of India soon — thank you for your patience as we grow.

  • Orders are processed within 1–2 business days of payment confirmation.
  • Delivery within Maharashtra typically takes 3–5 business days, depending on your location.
  • Shipping charges, if applicable, are calculated at checkout based on order weight and delivery pincode.
  • You will receive tracking details via SMS/email once your order is dispatched.
  • If your pincode falls outside our current serviceable area, this will be flagged at checkout before payment.
Last updated: August 2026

Refund & Return Policy

As SDK Masale products are food items (spices, masalas and cooking oils), we do not accept returns or exchanges once an order has been delivered, in line with standard food-safety practice.

  • Returns and refunds are not applicable for reasons such as change of mind or incorrect quantity ordered by the customer.
  • If you receive a damaged, leaking, or incorrect product, please contact us within 24 hours of delivery with photos of the item and packaging — we will review and resolve this on a case-by-case basis.
  • Approved resolutions may include a replacement or refund to the original payment method, at our discretion.
  • Refunds, where approved, are processed within 5–7 business days to the original payment method.
  • For any concerns, reach out to us at masalesdk@gmail.com or +91 96193 75636.

Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us while shopping on SDK Masale.

  • Information we collect: name, phone number, email, delivery address, and order details necessary to process and deliver your order.
  • How we use it: solely to process orders, communicate delivery updates, and improve our service. We do not sell or rent your personal information to third parties.
  • Payment information: payments are processed securely through our payment gateway partner; we do not store your card, UPI, or banking details on our servers.
  • Communication: we may contact you via SMS, WhatsApp, or email regarding your order status, and occasionally with offers — you can opt out of promotional messages anytime.
  • For any questions about how your data is handled, contact us at masalesdk@gmail.com.
